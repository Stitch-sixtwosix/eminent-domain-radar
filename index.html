Built by people who practice eminent domain

We are not a software company that discovered a legal niche. We are eminent domain professionals who needed this tool for our own cases and built it.

That is why it follows the way a taking actually unfolds: the planning list, the environmental review, the right-of-way plan, the offer, the declaration of taking. It ranks parcels the way a condemnation lawyer would, and every letter it sends to an owner is marked as attorney advertising.
