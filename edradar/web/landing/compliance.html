Compliance

Last updated November 17, 2026

EminentTracker helps attorneys write to people who may be affected by a public project. That is lawyer advertising, and it is regulated. This page lists the rules the service is run under, what the software does about each one, and what remains the subscribing attorney’s duty.

Lawyer advertising and solicitation

The rules. Each state’s Rules of Professional Conduct, based on ABA Model Rules 7.1 (truthful communications), 7.2 (advertising) and 7.3 (solicitation). In most states a lawyer may write to a prospective client but may not solicit by live telephone or in person, and written solicitations must be truthful and identify themselves as advertising. Some states add their own requirements, such as filing a copy with the bar, specific wording or type size, or record keeping.

What the software does. Every postcard carries the word “ADVERTISEMENT”, the firm’s name and address, and a statement that no taking has been announced. Letters and emails name the public record the contact is based on. The service offers mail and email only; it does not place calls or send text messages.

Your duty. Check the wording against the rules of your state and the recipient’s state before you send, file copies where your bar requires it, and keep copies for the period your rules set.

Email: CAN-SPAM Act

The rules. Commercial email must not use misleading headers or subject lines, must say it is an advertisement, must include the sender’s postal address, and must offer a way to opt out that is honored within 10 business days.

What the software does. Every letter and email ends with a line identifying it as an advertisement, the firm’s address and how to stop further contact. An owner marked “do not contact” is left out of every later campaign.

Your duty. Mark an owner “do not contact” as soon as they ask, and send only from a mailbox that belongs to your firm.

Telephone: TCPA and Do Not Call

The rules. The Telephone Consumer Protection Act and state laws restrict autodialed or prerecorded calls and text messages, and the National Do Not Call Registry bars telemarketing calls to listed numbers. Separately, Rule 7.3 bars most live telephone solicitation by lawyers.

What the software does. It does not dial or text. Phone numbers found by skip tracing are shown for reference only.

Your duty. Do not cold-call or text an owner. Use a phone number only to return contact the owner started, or where your rules clearly allow it.

Fair Credit Reporting Act and data-provider terms

The rules. EminentTracker is not a consumer reporting agency. Its records, including skip-traced contact details, may not be used to decide anyone’s eligibility for credit, insurance, employment, housing or any other purpose the FCRA covers. Skip-trace data is also subject to the provider’s permitted-use terms.

Your duty. Use records only to offer legal services to a property owner about a public project.

Mail: postal standards and deceptive mail

The rules. The Deceptive Mail Prevention and Enforcement Act forbids mail that looks like it comes from a government agency. Postal standards require a true return address.

What the software does. Postcards show the firm as the sender with its real return address and are never styled as an official notice.

Privacy laws and public-record terms

The rules. State privacy laws give people rights over personal information, and some states and counties limit commercial use of their public records. Idaho (Idaho Code 74-120), Montana (MCA 2-6-1017), South Carolina (S.C. Code 30-2-50) and Washington (RCW 42.56.070) restrict using government records for mailing lists or commercial solicitation, and some county files in Tennessee and elsewhere carry similar terms.

What the software does. Property owners can ask to be removed or not contacted at any time (see the privacy policy), and those requests are applied to every subscriber. Lead data is available only to signed-in subscribers. Where we know a law or an agency’s terms limit a file’s use for solicitation, we do not load its owner names or mailing addresses, so those places show projects and parcels without an owner.

Your duty. Before working in a new state or county, check whether it restricts using its records for solicitation. If you bring your own owner list for a restricted state, you are responsible for your right to use it, as the terms of use set out.

Use of artificial intelligence

The rules. Lawyers remain responsible for work done with technology (ABA Model Rules 1.1 on competence and 5.3 on supervising nonlawyer assistance, and state versions), and consumer-protection law forbids misleading claims about what automated tools can do.

What the software does. It says plainly that scores and classifications are produced by automated systems, shows the public source behind every project, and breaks each score into its parts so it can be checked. It does not draft legal advice and does not contact anyone on its own.

Your duty. Review a record and its source before acting on it, and review every communication before it goes out under your firm’s name.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe under the PCI Data Security Standard. EminentTracker does not see or store card numbers.
