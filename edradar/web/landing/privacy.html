Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

This policy explains what Duerr Imports, LLC (“EminentTracker”) collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information about subscribers

When you create an account we collect your name, work email, password (stored only in hashed form by our sign-in provider), firm name and location, phone number, website, chosen plan and states of practice. We use it to run your account, bill you and answer you. We do not sell it.

Information about property owners

The service shows records that government agencies publish: planned public projects, and for nearby parcels the owner of record, mailing address, parcel boundary and assessed value. Where a subscriber asks, we may also obtain a phone number or email address for an owner from a commercial data provider.

If you are a property owner and want your record removed from EminentTracker, or want subscribers to stop contacting you, email info@duerrlawpc.com with your name and the property address. We will suppress your record from outreach and confirm when it is done. This does not change the public record held by the county or agency.

Automated processing

We use automated systems, including artificial intelligence models, to read public project documents and score how likely a project is to need private land. These systems work on public records about projects and parcels. They are not used to make decisions about a person’s credit, employment, insurance or housing, and no decision with legal effect on a property owner is made by the software.

Companies that process data for us

  • Supabase: account sign-in and storage of the lead data
  • Stripe: subscription payments. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Lob: printing and mailing postcards
  • Apollo: business contact lookup and email sequences
  • BatchData: skip tracing, when a subscriber asks for it
  • GitHub Pages and Google Fonts: serving this website

Cookies

We use only the storage needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of certain uses. Email info@duerrlawpc.com and we will answer within 45 days. We do not sell personal information.

Keeping and securing data

Account data is kept while your account is open and deleted on request after it closes, except records we must keep for tax and legal reasons. Lead data is available only to signed-in subscribers.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will post the new version here and email subscribers.
