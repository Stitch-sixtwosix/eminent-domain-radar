Who may use the service

EminentTracker is for attorneys licensed in the United States and the staff who work under their supervision. You are responsible for everyone who uses your firm’s account.

What the service is, and is not

EminentTracker gathers public records about planned public projects and nearby property, and gives you tools to contact property owners. It is not a law firm. It does not give legal advice, does not create an attorney-client relationship with you or with any property owner, and does not refer clients for a fee. You are not charged per lead, per client or per result.

Accuracy of the records

Records come from public agencies and county offices and are passed on as published. They can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. A score is our estimate of how likely a project is to need private land; it is not a prediction that any property will be taken. Confirm ownership and project status with the source before you rely on a record.

Your responsibilities when you contact owners

  • Every letter, postcard and email sent through the service is your firm’s communication. You are responsible for its content and for following the advertising and solicitation rules of each state where you are licensed and where the recipient lives. See the compliance page.
  • You will not use the service to contact owners by live telephone call, text message or in person where your rules of professional conduct forbid it.
  • You will honor every request to stop contact and mark that owner “do not contact” in the service.
  • You will not use any record for a purpose covered by the Fair Credit Reporting Act, including decisions about credit, employment, insurance or housing.
  • You will not resell, republish or scrape the records, or share your account outside your firm.

State limits on using public records

Some states and counties limit the use of their public records for mailing lists or other commercial solicitation. They include Idaho (Idaho Code 74-120), Montana (MCA 2-6-1017) and Washington (RCW 42.56.070), along with some county files in Tennessee and other states, and the list changes. EminentTracker does not supply owner names or mailing addresses from a government file where we know such a limit applies. That is why some places show a project and its parcels with no owner.

If you get owner information for those places yourself, from an agency, a data vendor or anywhere else, and use it with the service, you are responsible for having the right to use it for solicitation. You will not import, upload or mail to a list that a law or the source’s terms forbid you to use that way. Records we do supply remain subject to the terms of the agency that published them, and you agree to follow those terms.

Automated and AI-assisted analysis

EminentTracker uses automated systems, including artificial intelligence models, to read public documents, classify projects, match them to parcels and assign scores. These results are produced without a lawyer reviewing each record. They can be wrong. They are a starting point for your own professional judgment, not a substitute for it, and you must verify a record before you act on it or contact anyone because of it.

You are the sender

When you send a postcard, letter or email through the service, your firm is the advertiser and the sender. EminentTracker supplies the tool and the template; it does not review, approve or send communications on its own behalf, and it does not decide whom you contact.

  • Harassing, threatening or misleading any person, or implying that a taking has been decided when it has not.
  • Using the service on behalf of anyone who is not a licensed attorney, or to find people for a purpose other than offering legal services about a public project.
  • Copying the data in bulk, reselling it, or using it to build a competing product.
